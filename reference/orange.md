# Orange

Spherical Ranges and Parametrization of Geographic Shapes

## Details

A collation of tools and metrics to characterize distribution data on
the surface of a sphere or an ellipsoid. The primary group of these
metrics is those that describe the extent of a distribution (geographic
ranges). The calculation of geographic ranges can be executed using
point coordinates data, vector polygons, as well as cells on a
discretized sphere. Besides ensuring the use a geometrically correct
implementations, the package offers the exploration of partial results
for visual diagnostics. This is still the pre-alpha version. Notes about
found bugs and suggestions are more than welcome!
