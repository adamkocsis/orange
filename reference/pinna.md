# Occurrences of the genus Pinna from OBIS

Occurrence records downloaded from OBIS on 2026-07-01

## Usage

``` r
data(pinna)
```

## Format

A `data.frame` with 1466 observations and 6 variables:

- `scientificName`:

  Taxon name.

- `species`:

  Species name.

- `basisOfRecord`:

  The basis of the record.

- `year`:

  Year.

- `depth`:

  Depth in meters.

- `decimalLongitude`:

  Longitude in decimal degrees.

- `decimalLatitude`:

  Latitude in decimal degrees.
